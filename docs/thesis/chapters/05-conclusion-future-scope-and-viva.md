## 5.3 Likely Viva Defense Q&A Bank

### Q: Why is formal non-equivalence alone insufficient to certify an ambiguity case?

**A:** Formal non-equivalence establishes different observable behavior under the declared model, environment, and observables. It does not establish that both interpretations are reasonable readings of the requirement. Certification also requires each reference to be valid and conform to its specification and both interpretations to pass the predefined human-plausibility protocol.

### Q: How does the project prevent post-hoc bias?

**A:** The intended safeguard is to version and hash requirements, interpretations, specifications, references, formal assumptions, and evidence, then freeze case membership, labels, annotations, models, prompts, settings, metrics, and denominators before final analysis. Any later change should be dated and marked as pre- or post-outcome inspection. The thesis must distinguish protocol requirements from completed case-specific freezes; the current model-evaluation infrastructure is still a draft and no model-performance result is established.
